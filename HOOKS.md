# Therm's 30-Hook Bank

The Brain agent picks every cover hook from this bank, then finishes it with a true,
specific NJ first-time-buyer payoff. Source: Therm's saved hook bank (Drive photos IMG_2626 to IMG_2630).

Rules:
- Use a different hook type for each of the 3 posts in a day.
- Do not reuse the same hook number within 7 days (check the Content Log, column "Hook #").
- The finished hook must be true for the reader. No hype, no claims a source does not support.
- Contrarian hooks (13 to 18) only when the slide content actually corrects a common belief.
- Keep it plain-language and neutral. Never insult buyers, sellers or other agents.

## WHAT MAKES A HOOK WORK (Therm's standard, Oct 22 2026; read before writing any hook)

Therm's call: "Don't make these 5 mistakes" is TRASH. "It took me 6 years to learn how to avoid
these 5 home buyer mistakes" is the standard. Every cover hook must pass this test:

1. PROOF: why should they listen to Therm? Use his real experience: "It took me 6 years to
   learn...", "After helping NJ buyers for 6 years...", "I've watched buyers lose deals over this."
   (Facts to use: 6 years in real estate, investing and sales; 15 years in a corporate career
   before that. Never inflate them, and never invent a client story or a dollar amount.)
2. A SPECIFIC NUMBER: "5 mistakes", "3 towns", "$15,000", "24 hours". Not "some" or "a few".
3. A COST OR STAKE: what they lose by not swiping (time, money, the house they wanted).
   "...that cost buyers the house", "...before you lose $10,000" (only if a source backs the number).
4. A CURIOSITY GAP: the hook promises the answer but doesn't give it. The payoff is on the slides.
5. A REAL PERSON TALKING: first person, said the way Therm would say it out loud. Not a
   textbook title, not a command shouted at the reader.

Instant rejects (the Quality gate scores any of these 6 or lower):
- A bare command with no proof or stakes: "Don't make these 5 mistakes", "Stop doing this",
  "Read this before you buy".
- A label instead of a hook: "Buyer mistakes", "Closing costs explained", "NJ attorney review".
- Words nobody says out loud: "essential", "crucial", "navigate", "journey", "unlock".

Weak -> strong (use the structure, write new words each time):
- "Don't make these 5 mistakes" -> "It took me 6 years to learn how to avoid these 5 home buyer mistakes"
- "Closing costs explained" -> "Nobody told my first buyers about these 4 costs until closing day"
- "What is attorney review?" -> "Your offer got accepted. Here's why the house still isn't yours yet"
- "3 coffee spots in Montclair" -> "I've shown homes in Montclair for years. These are the 3 coffee spots I send buyers to"
  (only if Therm has actually done this; otherwise "Moving to Montclair? Start with these 3 coffee spots")
- "You don't need 20% down" -> "I used to think you needed 20% down too. Here's what buyers actually put down"
  (careful: only claims that are true for Therm)

The 30 openers below are starting structures. Combine them with rules 1 to 5; never use one bare.
The Brain writes 3 hook options per post and picks the strongest; the checker scores the hook
separately, and a hook under 9 gets rewritten.
