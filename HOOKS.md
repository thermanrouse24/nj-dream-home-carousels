# Therm's 30-Hook Bank

The Brain agent picks every cover hook from this bank, then finishes it with a true,
specific NJ first-time-buyer payoff. Source: Therm's saved hook bank (Drive photos IMG_2626 to IMG_2630).

Rules:
- Use a different hook type for each of the 3 posts in a day.
- Do not reuse the same hook number within 7 days (check the Content Log, column "Hook #").
- The finished hook must be true for the reader. No hype, no claims a source does not support.
- Contrarian hooks (13 to 18) only when the slide content actually corrects a common belief.
- Keep it plain-language and neutral. Never insult buyers, sellers or other agents.

## Curiosity
1. No one talks about this but...
2. Here's the truth about...
3. This will change how you see...
4. Here's a secret you wish you knew sooner.
5. Here's what actually happens when you...
6. This is the part they never show you.

## Negative
7. Never, ever, ever...
8. This is why you're still...
9. Stop doing this.
10. I wish I stopped doing this sooner.
11. I wish someone warned me about this.
12. This mistake is costing you more than you think.

## Contrarian

14. Nobody wants to admit this but...
15. If this offends you...
16. The biggest lie you've been told about...
17. Everyone gets this wrong but...
18. The uncomfortable truth about...

## Experience

20. I used to believe this but...
21. This is what nobody tells beginners.
22. If I could only give one piece of advice about this...
23. This is the advice I ignored for years.
24. Nobody prepared me for this but...

## Call-out
25. If you only remember one thing about this...
26. You don't need more motivation, you need...
27. The problem isn't you, it's...
28. Stop scrolling if you care about...
29. You're probably doing this without realizing.
30. If you're struggling with this, listen.
